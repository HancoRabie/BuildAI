BuildAI ARCHITECTURAL BEST PRACTICES v1.1 — Minecraft Java 1.21.1
This is a requirements checklist, NOT optional cosmetic guidance. Resolve issues in geometry
before returning the complete JSON. Structure must be playable, not merely photogenic.

1. PROGRAM & SCALE
- Begin with a mental space program: externally recognizable elements, named rooms,
  halls, entrances, stairs, basement, landscape and connections between sections.
- One horizontal block is approximately 1 meter; avatar height is ~1.8 blocks.
- Typical traversable entrance is at least two clear blocks tall; premium hallways
  should be three or more, with width appropriate to the building.
- Floors, ceilings and roofs must be continuous except at deliberate stairs, shafts
  and atriums. Never let unplanned dirt/grass appear inside finished rooms.
- Do not invent finished rooms by naming them only; physically include them in operations.

2. STAIRS, ACCESSIBILITY & VERTICAL CIRCULATION
- Design stairs BEFORE placing ceiling slabs or surrounding walls.
- Every staircase connects two usable LANDINGS at distinct floor elevations.
  A flight must not terminate at a solid wall, glass, ceiling, or unsupported edge.
- Stair orientation uses correct facing, half=bottom (unless deliberately inverted),
  shape and waterlogged states. Test each step as an accessible climb of one block
  rise in the block grid, with TWO unobstructed blocks of headroom where a player stands.
- Cut a correctly sized opening in the upper floor for the ENTIRE flight and landing.
  Ensure supporting floor under each landing and finished walls around the stairwell.
- Provide a safe corridor at both ends; do not leave open grass or a pit beside steps.
- For basements, connect stairwell to the basement level and finish underground walls
  and ceilings so excavated soil is not exposed in indoor living areas.

3. ROOM CONNECTIVITY & DOORWAYS
- Every intentionally accessible named room must connect to a main entrance through
  a two-block-high doorway and a connected corridor/stair network; check turns.
- Corridors must not narrow to blocked one-cell choke points around furniture.
- Doors need upper/lower halves and the correct hinge/facing. Carve doorway air FIRST
  and position the door AFTER wall generation. Door upper half must have clearance.
- Furniture must not block entrances, stairs or the walking path between rooms.
- Atriums and floor voids must be intentional; rail or enclose unsafe edges.

4. WINDOWS, LIGHTING & FURNISHINGS
- Windows should be aligned and framed; glass panes must connect to their neighbors.
- Light every main hall, stairwell, basement and room, not only the exterior facade.
- Choose furniture placement after the walkable route is confirmed.
- Avoid unsupported glass panes, floating lights, and floating stairs.

5. HISTORICALLY NAMED BUILDINGS
- A White House replica needs its recognisable central residence, porticos,
  East and West Wings, formal approach, and actual room layout approximations.
- REQUIRED FOR A WHITE HOUSE REQUEST: physically build a recognizable OVAL OFFICE
  in the West Wing. Use a stepped elliptical/rounded room outline, furnished
  presidential desk area, glazing and a usable doorway connected to the West Wing.
  Clearly include an entry named 'Oval Office' in the room manifest; don't imply
  documentary accuracy if dimensions or internal layout are approximations.
- For any requested landmark, include ALL specifically named rooms. Do not omit them
  to save output tokens without telling the user.

6. ROOM MANIFEST FOR MACHINE CHECKING
- Include an OPTIONAL top-level "rooms" array in the design JSON:
  "rooms":[{"name":"Oval Office","x1":3,"y1":2,"z1":3,"x2":14,"y2":6,"z2":13}]
- Each room bounding box should cover its physical room walls, floor and headroom.
  Name actual recognizable locations, not imagined placeholders.
- Coordinate values must stay within the blueprint dimensions.
- BuildAI will inspect basic two-block headroom and openings near claimed rooms;
  that heuristic is NOT an exhaustive pathfinding or accessibility certification.

7. FINAL INTERNAL QA BEFORE OUTPUT
- X horizontal, Y vertical, Z depth; honor exact block states and bounding dimensions.
- Trace a player route: outdoors → primary door → main hall → each room →
  ascending/descending staircase → upper floor and basement. Repair broken connections.
- Trace the cutout of every staircase in both vertical X and Z cross-sections.
- Check floor continuity and interior finish around every void.
- Emit COMPLETE, valid ordered operations; 'air' operations must create real spaces.
- Avoid claims of a fully functional machine or verified room accessibility without tests.

8. DOOR PLACEMENT & HINGE QA
- Fit every wooden or iron door into an intentionally cut 1x2 opening, never into a 1-high
  slot or embedded in an adjacent solid panel. A standard door uses exactly two block
  states sharing (X,Z): half=lower at the floor and half=upper one level above.
- Facing and hinge must match across both halves; set open=false and powered=false
  unless the design calls for a specific door state. Single-width doors should close
  into their jamb. Two-wide openings need either two coordinated doors or a deliberate
  door-free archway. Do NOT leave a lonely off-center door in a broad opening.
- Include walkable full-height clearance for the player immediately on BOTH sides;
  furniture and tables do not belong in the door swing or landing.
- If an upper door half is missing and its cell is empty, BuildAI can add it safely;
  colliding geometry is warned about, never silently replaced.

9. CONNECTED RAILINGS & NATURAL MATERIALS
- Balconies, stairs and decks should have continuous railings (especially open edges)
  and corners, with consistent fence or wall material rather than scattered fence posts.
  BuildAI recomputes their north/east/south/west connections on final placement.
- Prefer glass panes in upright window walls where panes preserve the intended
  proportions; panes need neighboring panes or framing on the correct sides.
  Solid glass blocks remain appropriate for skylights, domes, flush roofs, and
  special facade treatments, so do not globally substitute glass types.
- For premium architecture, use contrast between structural timber/log beams, wooden
  plank surfaces, trimmed facades, supports, railing, glazing and furnishings,
  rather than arbitrarily scattering random block palettes.

10. ORGANIC TREES AND JUNGLE CANOPIES
- Build tapered visible log trunks with distinct roots and multiple asymmetric
  branch lines that reach the leaf clusters and support the crown visually.
- Use overlapping organic_canopy operations, each with x1/y1/z1/x2/y2/z2,
  a valid leaves block, and an optional seed integer, to create uneven
  ellipsoidal foliage clumps; adjust cluster sizes across crown levels.
- NO giant axis-aligned rectangular leaf roof, no perfectly flat crown,
  and no unsupported levitating foliage disconnected from the branch system.
- Leave believable gaps for sun and windows; vary height and density of clusters.
- For treehouses, roof/branches should integrate so the dwelling looks supported
  by the tree rather than a house with a flat leaf box hovering above it.
